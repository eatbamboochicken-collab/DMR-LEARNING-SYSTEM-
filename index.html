THE BOOM

Primary Vertical Structural Beam & Hydraulic Lift Geometry

WORK EQUIPMENT
📌 WHAT IS IT?

The boom is the large curved structural arm pinned directly to the revolving upperstructure of the excavator. It is fabricated from high-strength reinforced box-section steel.

⚙️ WHAT DOES IT DO?

Powered by two massive dual hydraulic lift cylinders, the boom raises and lowers the entire front work attachment. It provides vertical lift reach and positions the arm and bucket over deep trenches or high haul truck beds.

🎯 WHY DOES IT MATTER?

The boom is the foundation of all digging geometry. Misusing the boom to violently pry boulders or levering against solid bedrock can bend hydraulic cylinder rods or crack main structural pivot welds.

OPERATOR MEMORY RULE
REMEMBER: BOOM → ARM (STICK) → BUCKET

Always think outward from the machine centerline. The Boom provides elevation, the Arm provides pulling force (crowd), and the Bucket provides breakout cutting force.
